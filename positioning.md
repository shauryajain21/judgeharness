## Core angles / taglines

1. **Ditch the provider you chose in 2022. There's a new SOTA.**
   The landscape moved; your vendor choice didn't. What was best three years ago
   almost certainly isn't best today.

2. **Your production traffic has changed significantly — it deserves something new.**
   Your traffic distribution today ≠ what it was when you picked your stack (and ≠
   lab benchmarks). Re-evaluate against *your* current reality.

3. **Get faster, better, and spend less — just by switching vendors.**
   Same product, better components. Quality up, latency down, cost down — no
   re-architecting required.
